# English AI tells

Same three-layer logic as the Chinese list. Use this when the draft is in English or mixes English into Chinese.

## Phrases to cut

**Throat-clearing openers**: In today's fast-paced world / In an era where / It's worth noting that / Let's dive into / When it comes to / At its core.

**Emphasis crutches**: truly, deeply, incredibly, remarkably, fundamentally, seamlessly, effortlessly, robust, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionary, transformative, unlock, leverage, harness, elevate, empower, delve, tapestry, landscape (as an abstract noun), testament to, navigate the complexities of.

**Hedges stacked together**: may potentially, somewhat generally, in some ways, it could be argued that. Keep one hedge per sentence at most.

**Meta-commentary**: This article will explore / As mentioned earlier / In conclusion / Hope this helps / Let me know if you'd like me to expand.

**Vague attribution**: experts say, studies show, it is widely believed — with no source. Cite it, cut it, or own it as your own judgment.

## Structures to break

| Pattern | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Rule of three | fast, reliable, and secure | Keep the one that's true and prove it |
| Negative parallelism | It's not just X — it's Y | Max once per piece |
| Rhetorical setup | The question is: can we? | Just answer it |
| Binary contrast | While some argue X, others argue Y | State your read |
| False agency | The data reveals / The complaint becomes a fix | Give it a human subject |
| Dramatic fragmentation | Three words. Then a fragment. For effect. | Rare, not rhythmic |
| Em dash overuse | Two or more per paragraph | Periods or commas |
| Title Case Headings | Why This Matters For Your Team | Sentence case |
| Uniform paragraph length | Every block 3–4 lines | Vary deliberately |

## Sentence-level rules

- Active voice with a human subject doing something, unless the actor genuinely doesn't matter.
- Cut adverbs first — most modify a weak verb that should be replaced instead.
- No Wh- openers as a habit (Whether you're a beginner or…).
- Specific nouns over category nouns: "the 4am pager alert" beats "operational challenges".
- Vary sentence length across any three consecutive sentences.

## Voice

The clean draft still needs a person in it: a take, an admitted cost, a concrete scene, first person where honest, and an ending that stops on a fact or an open question rather than a lift.

## False positives

Technical docs, legal text, abstracts, and incident reports have their own register. Passive voice and formal diction are correct there. Quoted material, data, and terminology stay untouched — repeating the same term for the same concept is precision, not repetition.
